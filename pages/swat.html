Swat Valley

The Switzerland of Pakistan — emerald valleys, rivers, and ancient ruins

About Swat Valley

Swat Valley in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has been called the Switzerland of Pakistan for good reason. Lush green mountains, crystal-clear rivers, and ancient Buddhist ruins make it one of Asia's most underrated destinations. Home to Malam Jabba — Pakistan's premier ski resort — Swat offers year-round appeal from spring wildflowers to winter snow.
