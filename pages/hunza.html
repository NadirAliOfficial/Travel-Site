Hunza Valley

Heaven on Earth — glaciers, ancient forts, and the roof of the world

About Hunza Valley

Hunza Valley in Gilgit-Baltistan is widely considered Pakistan's most spectacular destination. Surrounded by seven peaks over 6,000 metres, including Rakaposhi (7,788m) and Ultar Sar, the valley offers ancient Silk Road forts, turquoise Attabad Lake, and cherry blossom seasons that rival Japan's. The people of Hunza are known worldwide for their longevity and hospitality.
