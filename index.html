Why choose Pakistan

A World Within One Country

Pakistan holds more natural and cultural wonders than most people can imagine.

🏔

World's Greatest Mountains

Five of the world's 14 eight-thousanders are in Pakistan, including K2 — the world's hardest peak.

🏛

5,000 Years of History

From the ancient Indus Valley civilization at Mohenjo-daro to Mughal masterpieces in Lahore.

🍛

Incredible Food Culture

Pakistani cuisine is ranked among the world's finest — from Lahori nihari to Hunza's dried fruits.

🌿

Unspoiled Natural Beauty

Deserts, glaciers, rainforests, and beaches — Pakistan has more ecosystems than almost any country.
